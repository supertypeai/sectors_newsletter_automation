# Writing, newsletter surface (runtime entry)

**Read `./writing/core.md` first, top to bottom.** It carries the non-negotiables, the editorial laws and the arc, plain words, the AI-tell filter, the rhythm ban, directness and the voice. All of it binds here. This file adds only what is specific to a newsletter, and it may tighten a core rule but never relax one.

Alongside it: `./writing/brand-voice.md` (the voice in full) and `./writing/audit.md` (the gate
before delivery). Those three are static reference copies of rules shared across every Sectors
content skill, pulled in so this skill runs standalone — see each file's own header for where
the canonical version lives and how to re-pull it.

**The unit here is one section.** Every "unit" rule in the core applies per section: a reader who jumps straight to the section that interests them must be able to read it without the ones above it.

This skill has no cover slide and no Instagram caption. The core's hook rule (§2, move 6) lands on the **subject line** and the **opening line**, both governed by `./newsletter-format.md`.

---

## 1. What overrides what

The core is written medium-neutrally, but a newsletter is read as continuous prose rather than glanced at as fragments. Two places where that matters:

**Sentences run longer here.** The core's plain-words rules stay, and the rhythm ban in core §5 stays and matters more, not less. A newsletter section reduced to a stack of clipped subject-verb-object statements is the loudest machine tell in this medium. Let sentences carry two related facts through to a conclusion with a conjunction instead of chopping everything into fragments for punch. Full treatment in `./newsletter-format.md`'s **Prose style** section, which is required reading before drafting any issue.

**The dash ban covers the whole file, not just the body.** No em dash, en dash or spaced hyphen anywhere: Sources, the Appendix, table cells, captions and alt text included. In delivered HTML this means no `&mdash;`, no `&ndash;` and no literal dash character in the file. Between a source link and what it backs, use a colon. A hyphen inside a compound word or a numeric range (`62-65Mt/yr`, `year-on-year`) is not a connector and stays.

## 2. Newsletter-specific craft

Everything below is format rather than voice, and lives in `./newsletter-format.md`. Read it; do not re-derive it here.

- Subject line and opening line
- Section headings that state the finding rather than naming the slot
- Ticker-mention convention, UTM convention, number formatting
- Bite-sized and visual formatting, and the required visual per issue type
- Section skeletons per issue type, and length guidance
- The upcoming-events closing block, the data-source appendix, the standard disclaimer

Sourcing rules for anything web-sourced are in `./sourcing.md`. No source, no claim.

Run `./writing/audit.md` in full. On top of it, newsletter-specific:

Every section readable alone · headings state the finding, not the slot · no dash anywhere in the delivered file, body or chrome · the required visual present · appendix mapping every metric to its endpoint and field path · every web claim cited · disclaimer footer present.
