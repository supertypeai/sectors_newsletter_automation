ICBP Sales Rose 15% in Nine Months While Net Profit Fell 13%

Indofood CBP Sukses Makmur sells instant noodles, dairy and snacks to a mass market. Instant noodles supply nearly three quarters of its revenue. In the nine months to September 2026, sales grew while net profit attributable to owners shrank. Operating profit still rose, and management raised full-year sales guidance.

At a Glance
  • Net profit: Profit attributable to owners fell -13.2% to IDR 6.16T in nine months while sales rose +15% to IDR 64.45T (Fortune IDN, 2 Nov 2026).
  • Price: ICBP closed at IDR 6,200 on 9 November, -17.9% since 12 August and +13.2% above its 30 October low of IDR 5,475.
  • Profitability: 2025 gross margin was 35.2% against 37.0% in 2023, with return on equity at 12.5% against 18.9% in 2019.
  • Core business: Instant noodles are 72.7% of 2025 revenue, IDR 54.38T of IDR 74.85T.
  • Open Question: Do currency losses on the company's dollar bonds reverse with the rupiah, or will IDR 49.92T of debt continue to weigh on net profit as sales grow?

The share price fell from IDR 7,950 on 27 August to IDR 5,475 on 30 October, then recovered to IDR 6,200 by 9 November.

Sales Guidance Raised Despite Lower Net Profit

ICBP's nine-month results brought two contrasting signals. Management raised full-year sales growth guidance to 12% to 14% from 5% to 7% and kept its operating margin target at 20% to 22% (Investor.id, 3 Nov 2026).

Profit attributable to owners fell 13.2% to IDR 6.16T. Core profit, which strips out foreign exchange and one-off items, rose 5% (Fortune IDN, 2 Nov 2026).

The stock hit a 90 day low of IDR 5,475 on 30 October and gained 15.5% by 6 November as the results came out.

Quarterly numbers below run through the second quarter, while nine-month figures are company-reported.

ICBP relies on one main product category, supported by four smaller segments.

Scale and Distribution Protect Margins

The protection here is scale and a distribution network, and the test is whether margins held when costs rose.

ICBP holds the highest market cap among its industry peers. Its largest independent competitor, MYOR, earns roughly a third of ICBP's net profit and trades at a higher price-to-book (P/B) ratio of 1.59x versus 1.37x. Selling and distribution forms the largest operating expense at 10.6% of 2025 revenue.

INDF is the group's parent and consolidates ICBP, so the two are not independent competitors (Industry.co.id, 2 Nov 2026). Gross margin stayed between 33.6% and 37.0% from 2020 to 2025, a band of under four points through a period when the company reports rising input costs.

Return metrics tell a different story. Return on equity (ROE) was 18.9% in 2019 and has ranged between 8.0% and 13.1% since 2020, when total equity nearly doubled. Margins held firm, but shareholder returns have not recovered.

Dollar Debt Exposes ICBP to Rupiah Weakness

Three macroeconomic factors influence earnings, with currency movements impacting net profit most directly.

Rupiah. The rupiah traded near 18,160 per dollar on 2 November and remained above 18,000 for a second week (Trading Economics, 2 Nov 2026). Nine-month results show higher foreign exchange losses tied directly to ICBP's dollar bonds (Industry.co.id, 2 Nov 2026).

Interest rates. Bank Indonesia raised key interest rates by 100 basis points across May and June (Trading Economics, 2 Nov 2026) before holding the BI-Rate at 5.75% on 21 October (Bank Indonesia, 21 Oct 2026). Financing costs reduced net profit (Fortune IDN, 2 Nov 2026).

Input costs. Raw materials, packaging, and shipping pressed nine-month operating margins down 1.4 percentage points to 21.2% (Investor.id, 3 Nov 2026). Cost of revenue represented 64.8% of 2025 sales.

The business generated IDR 15.67T in 2025 operating income behind steady distribution and scale. Non-operating costs, foreign currency fluctuations, and interest expenses caused the squeeze on final earnings.

A dividend of IDR 265 a share went ex-dividend in July, a 4.3% yield against a five year average of 2.6%.

The counterargument is that the weakness sits below the operating line and the operating line is growing. Operating profit rose 7% on 15% more sales (Fortune IDN, 2 Nov 2026), guidance moved up, and the analyst revenue estimate for 2026 implies growth of 15.0% over 2025.

Of 72 analysts on sectors.app, 59 rate the shares buy and 13 hold, as of 3 November, and none rate them sell.

Currency movements on ICBP's dollar bonds could swing net profit, while IDR 49.92T in total debt continues to demand interest service as sales grow.
What to Watch Next
  • Fourth quarter and full year 2026 results. Nine-month sales growth of 15% sits above the raised 12% to 14% guidance, and the 20% to 22% operating margin range is the second guided reading to check.
  • Rupiah exchange rate around 18,000 per dollar. Unrealized losses on ICBP's dollar bonds move with the currency, making November exchange rates critical to upcoming earnings charges.
  • Interest coverage ratios. Interest coverage fell to 4.1x in 2025 from 7.3x in 2023. A move back toward 2023 levels would confirm debt service costs are stabilizing.
  • Seasonings market share. Seasonings revenue grew 27.2% over two years to hit 4.8% of total sales. Reaching 5% would mark the first significant revenue mix shift in years.
