Three Majority Holders Raised Their Stakes This Week
The Three Filings
DGIK Nusa Konstruksi Enjiniring
Global Dinamika Kencana filed three purchases dated 6, 9 and 10 November, all at IDR 135, lifting its stake from 52.30% to 62.40%. The 560.51 million shares cost IDR 75.67B.
IATA MNC Energy Investments
Karya Pacific Investama bought 1.23 billion shares on 3 November at IDR 66, filed on 11 November, taking its stake from 62.47% to 66.40%. The purchase cost IDR 81.04B.
NRCA Nusa Raya Cipta
Surya Semesta Internusa bought 14 million shares on 9 November at IDR 390, lifting its stake from 63.94% to 64.50%. The purchase cost IDR 5.46B.
Why a Majority Holder's Buying Matters
What it can signal

A holder above 50% already decides every ordinary shareholder vote, so each extra share is more capital committed to a company it already steers. Holders sit close to contracts and cash flow, which makes repeated buying worth noting, though a single filing can also reflect a change in how shares are held.

What it can change

Each purchase takes shares away from other investors, so the portion held by everyone else shrinks. A smaller outside holding can mean thinner trading, and it can matter for resolutions that need more than a simple majority of votes.

The filings say who bought, how many shares and at what price. They do not say why, and they do not say what the share price will do next.
