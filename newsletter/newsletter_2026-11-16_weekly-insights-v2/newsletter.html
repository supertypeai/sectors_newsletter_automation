Market breakdown
LQ45 rose 6.19% while total market cap rose 3.47%
• Most of the gain arrived in the last two sessions: LQ45 added 3.12% on Thursday 12 November, the session after MSCI's November review results came out, and 1.35% on Friday. Together these account for 4.51% of the move from a Wednesday close that stood 1.61% above the prior Friday.
• Buying concentrated in the most liquid stocks. LQ45's gain was nearly double the expansion in total market cap.
• Index changes hit the next-weakest names. BRPT fell -5.29% during the week MSCI dropped it from its Global Standard indexes, while MAPI fell -5.10% after the exchange removed it from LQ45 following its shareholding-concentration review.
ADRO rose 7.36% and AADI rose 5.42% in the week Adaro Strategic sold out of both
• One seller left both companies on 10 November as four buyers stepped in. Adaro Strategic Investments sold its entire 48.77% stake in ADRO and 41.10% in AADI, worth IDR 33.89T and IDR 32.92T (IDR 66.81T total). Triputra Investindo Arya, Saratoga Investama Sedaya, Persada Capital Investama and Trinugraha Thohir each filed purchases across both entities.
• No single entity took a controlling block. The four buyers now hold between 8.86% and 16.86% of the two companies, with Saratoga Investama Sedaya holding the largest positions at 16.86% of ADRO and 15.15% of AADI.
• The transaction caused little immediate price disruption. ADRO moved up +0.43% and AADI fell -1.22% on 10 November. Main gains picked up Thursday, when ADRO gained +6.06% on 51 million shares, its highest daily volume for the week.
VKTR rose 52.59% and SINI rose 43.46%, both far above their latest rights prices
• The week's top gainers are trading well past recent rights pricing. VKTR closed at IDR 354 compared to its IDR 200 rights price (ex-date 7 October), up 77%. SINI finished at IDR 10,975 versus IDR 5,000 (ex-date 9 July), roughly 2.2 times higher.
• VKTR's move was a bounce in an ongoing decline. The stock had dropped from IDR 400 on 23 October (the end of rights trading) to IDR 230 on 9 November, so it remains 11.5% below that earlier high.
• Volume accelerated into Friday for both stocks. VKTR traded 912.8 million shares while SINI turned over 21.0 million shares (1.7 times its Wednesday level).

Summary
The bull read
• Broad participation across large caps drove the advance, with top constituents outperforming the index. That institutional focus gives the market a solid foundation.
• Core bank earnings are growing consistently at BBRI and BMRI, protecting the index's largest sector from total reliance on foreign capital.
• Domestic buyers cleanly absorbed the major Adaro block sale, with ADRO and AADI closing higher. Domestic balance sheets continue to take down sizable equity stakes.
The bear read
• A weak currency narrows a central bank's room to ease, so funding costs for banks such as BBRI and BMRI stay elevated into the rate decision.
• The index-tracking selling is still ahead, because the MSCI removals take effect only at the end of the month and the shareholding-concentration review keeps reshaping index membership, so mechanical demand for the weakest names has yet to show up in prices.
• The top of the movers table is dominated by thinly traded names priced far above their last rights offers, which is speculative flow that can reverse as quickly as it arrived.
What to watch next
Track price action and volume in BRPT into the 30 November close:
• If BRPT handles index fund selling with minimal price impact, it validates institutional support across main board equities.
• If BRPT experiences heavy selling that drags down other LQ45 names, market vulnerability to passive outflows increases.
