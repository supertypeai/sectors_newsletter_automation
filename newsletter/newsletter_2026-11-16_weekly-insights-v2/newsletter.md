## What the Data Unearthed

### LQ45 rose 6.19% while total market cap rose 3.47%

- Most of the gain arrived in the last two sessions: LQ45 added 3.12% on Thursday 12 November, the session after MSCI's November review results came out, and 1.35% on Friday, which together make up 4.51% of the move from a Wednesday close that stood only 1.61% above the prior Friday.
- The largest names carried the rally, since LQ45's gain was nearly twice the gain in total market cap, which says the buying concentrated in the most liquid stocks.
- The index changes left marks on the next-weakest names: [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=brpt) fell **-5.29%** in the week MSCI removed it from its Global Standard indexes, and [**MAPI**](https://sectors.app/idx/mapi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=mapi) fell **-5.10%** in the week the exchange dropped it from LQ45 after its shareholding-concentration review.

### [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=adro) rose 7.36% and [**AADI**](https://sectors.app/idx/aadi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=aadi) rose 5.42% in the week Adaro Strategic sold out of both

- One seller left both companies on 10 November and the same four buyers took over: Adaro Strategic Investments sold its entire 48.77% of [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=adro) and 41.10% of [**AADI**](https://sectors.app/idx/aadi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=aadi), worth IDR 33.89T and IDR 32.92T, or IDR 66.81T together, and Triputra Investindo Arya, Saratoga Investama Sedaya, Persada Capital Investama and Trinugraha Thohir each filed purchases in both.
- No single holder replaced the departing block, since each of the four now holds between 8.86% and 16.86% of the two companies, with Saratoga Investama Sedaya the largest at 16.86% of [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=adro) and 15.15% of [**AADI**](https://sectors.app/idx/aadi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=aadi).
- The handover left no footprint on the day, with [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=adro) up **+0.43%** and [**AADI**](https://sectors.app/idx/aadi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=aadi) down **-1.22%** on 10 November, and the gains came on Thursday, when [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=adro) rose **+6.06%** on 51 million shares, its heaviest volume of the week.

## Summary

**The bull read**

- The rally reached almost every large cap and the biggest names outran the broad market, which says institutional demand returned where liquidity is deepest and gives the index a firmer base than a handful of speculative winners would.
- Earnings at the big banks keep compounding at [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) and [**BMRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bmri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bmri), which gives the heaviest sector in the index a profit base that does not depend on foreign flows.
- The Adaro group handover to four domestic holders went through with [**ADRO**](https://sectors.app/idx/adro?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=adro) and [**AADI**](https://sectors.app/idx/aadi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=aadi) both rising, a sign that large strategic blocks can still find domestic balance sheets to absorb them.

**The bear read**

- A weak currency narrows a central bank's room to ease, so funding costs for banks such as [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) and [**BMRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bmri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bmri) stay elevated into the rate decision.
- The index-tracking selling is still ahead, because the MSCI removals take effect only at the end of the month and the shareholding-concentration review keeps reshaping index membership, so mechanical demand for the weakest names has yet to show up in prices.
- The top of the movers table is dominated by thinly traded names priced far above their last rights offers, which is speculative flow that can reverse as quickly as it arrived.

**What to watch next**

Monitor price action and volume in [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=brpt) into the 30 November close, when the MSCI changes take effect:

- If [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=brpt) absorbs the index-fund selling with only modest price damage, it supports the bull read that the large-cap bid is durable.
- If [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-16&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=brpt) sells off hard into the effective date and the weakness spreads to other LQ45 names, it adds weight to the bear read on mechanical outflows.
