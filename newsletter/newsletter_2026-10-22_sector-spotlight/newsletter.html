Banks Fell 9.4% in a Week and Now Trade at a Five-Year Low of 8.0x Earnings

The 48 listed banks on IDX carry a combined market cap of IDR 2,075.32T and a median 2026 P/E of 8.0x, the lowest of the five yearly readings sectors.app carries for the group. Their combined market cap fell 9.4% over the past week and is down 30.9% year-to-date. The week's trigger was MSCI's warning that Indonesia could be removed from its global indexes next year (Trading Economics, 16 Oct 2026), a day on which the Jakarta Composite closed down 11.57% and the finance index fell 8.98% (Databoks, 16 Oct 2026).

The market-cap-weighted P/E is 16.1x, twice the 8.0x median. The four largest banks by market cap trade between 4.9x and 11.1x trailing earnings, below that weighted figure, so the gap points to smaller lenders with high multiples, among them Bank Permata (BNLI) at 17.3x, Bank Jago (ARTO) at 24.3x, Super Bank Indonesia (SUPA) at 64.7x and Krom Bank Indonesia (BBSI) at 68.2x (sectors.app).

Five of the six lowest multiples belong to state-owned or regional-government lenders: BJTM, BBTN, BJBR, BMRI and BBNI. Bank Central Asia (BBCA), the largest bank at IDR 643.77T, sits at 11.1x. CIMB Niaga Rose While the Big State Banks Fell, With Institutions Still Buying

Bank CIMB Niaga (BNGA) closed at IDR 1,725 on 21 October, up 2.7% on the week, after touching a 52-week low of IDR 1,435 on 19 October (sectors.app). It is one of only two screened banks that rose, and it is the one with a flow and balance-sheet record the data can compare against the three big state-linked banks.

On P/E, BNGA is third lowest of the four at 6.3x, below the 8.0x sub-sector median, while Bank Mandiri (BMRI) is lowest at 4.9x. BNGA carries the lowest debt/equity of the four and the highest capital adequacy ratio. Its ROE of 11.8% is the second lowest, and every bank in the table posted a lower ROE in 2025 than in 2024: BNGA from 13.0%, BMRI from 17.8%, BBNI from 12.8% and Bank Rakyat Indonesia (BBRI) from 19.0%.

Institutional flow cuts the same way as the price. Across the seven monthly readings sectors.app returned, ending 30 September, institutions were net buyers of BNGA in every month, net sellers of BMRI in six, and net sellers of BBNI and BBRI in all seven.

The forecasts are listed as published and may not yet reflect the past week's repricing.

Key Takeaway

The 8.0x median and the 16.1x weighted P/E describe two different groups of banks, a state-owned and regional cluster at the cheap end and a handful of high-multiple lenders at the other. The week's sell-off reached 23 of 25 screened banks, and it hit the highest-multiple names hardest.

BNGA stands apart on this screen: it rose 2.7% while the large state banks fell 4.7% to 9.1%, it carries the lowest debt/equity and highest capital adequacy ratio among the four compared, and institutions were net buyers in each monthly reading. Its ROE has slipped for a year, and its 6.3x multiple is not the lowest in the group.

The open question for the group is whether the forecast earnings recovery arrives while MSCI's possible reclassification of Indonesia stays unresolved through next year. This issue is valuation context only, and none of it is a call on any bank.
