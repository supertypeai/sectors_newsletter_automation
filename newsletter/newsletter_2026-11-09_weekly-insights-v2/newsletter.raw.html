What the Data Unearthed
BRPT fell 20.56% in seven days while BREN rose 17.55%, both with buybacks under way
• Both group companies run buybacks, and the shares still went opposite ways: BRPT had used about 12% of its IDR 1T ceiling and BREN about 10% of its own.
• BRPT closed at IDR 850 against IDR 1,440 on 22 October, a 40.97% fall in two weeks, and finished lower on four of this week's five sessions.
• Thursday was the busiest session of the two weeks for both names, 186.79M shares in BRPT and 43.10M in BREN, so both names saw their heaviest trading of the two weeks on the same day.
Four of the six best LQ45 performers were consumer staples, led by ICBP at +15.53%
• ICBP, UNVR, INDF and AMRT gained +15.53%, +8.99%, +7.42% and +6.56% while the LQ45 as a whole rose only +0.60%, so defensive earners supplied the index's best returns.
• ICBP rose on four of five sessions, and Friday's 14.95M shares were 71% above the prior Friday's 8.72M, which says buyers kept arriving as the price climbed.
• Analysts covering retailers called defensive operators such as AMRT more resilient than discretionary ones given weak purchasing power, and AMRT recovered 14.04% from Tuesday's close of IDR 855.
MDKA gained 8.89% in the week, with Thursday's jump coinciding with a rise in gold
• MDKA rose 15.76% on 5 November to IDR 2,130, the session gold climbed 1.6% to USD 4,125 an ounce, which more than accounts for the weekly gain.
• ANTM fell 6.98% on Tuesday to IDR 2,400 and closed Friday at IDR 2,690, above its prior close of IDR 2,620, so Thursday's rebound recovered the earlier sell-off.
• AMMN rose 8.28% on Thursday and still ended the week at -0.82%, because Tuesday and Wednesday together took 8.15% off.

Summary
The bull read
• Staples led the index higher while breadth stayed level, so capital is rotating toward defensive earners inside the market and leaving little sign of a wholesale exit from the large-cap indices.
• Issuer money is now on the bid in BRPT, BREN and GOTO, which gives the market a domestic buyer that does not depend on foreign flows during a year of heavy foreign outflows.
The bear read
• BRPT kept sliding with a live buyback behind it, which shows that issuer purchases at this scale are too small to offset heavy selling.
• Market capitalization fell for a second week while LQ45 and IDX30 rose, so the damage has moved down to mid and small caps where liquidity is thinner.
• Foreign selling concentrated in BBRI on the 3 November drop, and a weak rupiah keeps the currency cost of holding Indonesian banks high for offshore investors.
What to watch next
Monitor BBRI and BBCA price action and volume into the 11 November MSCI review:
• If BBRI and BBCA hold Friday's levels on steady volume through the review, it supports the bull read that large caps keep a domestic bid.
• If heavy selling returns to the bank heavyweights, with foreign net selling again concentrated in BBRI, it adds weight to the bear read on foreign exit.
