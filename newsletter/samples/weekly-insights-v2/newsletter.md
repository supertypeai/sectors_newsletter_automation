**Issue #28 | 13 July 2026 | week of 6-10 July | data as of 10 July 2026**

## Key Data Bites

- IDX total market cap closed at **IDR 10,336.48T**, up **+0.51% WoW**, less than half the
  prior week's +1.02%.
- The whole gain came from the weekend gap. The index opened Monday at IDR 10,352.61T and
  closed Friday at IDR 10,336.48T, **down 0.16% Monday to Friday**.
- **LQ45 +1.28%** and **IDX30 +1.36%** both beat the whole market's +0.51%, so the large caps
  carried an otherwise flat tape.

## What the Data Unearthed

### Basic Materials filled both ends of the table

![Weekly price change for four LQ45 Basic Materials names, 6-10 July 2026: BRPT +9.09%, ESSA +6.48%, MBMA -5.00%, MDKA -4.81%](chart-basic-materials-split.svg)

- Four of the ten biggest LQ45 movers share one sector classification, **Basic Materials**,
  and they sit at opposite ends of it.
- Up: [$BRPT](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=brpt) **+9.09%** and
  [$ESSA](https://sectors.app/idx/essa?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=essa) **+6.48%**, both petrochemical processors.
- Down: [$MBMA](https://sectors.app/idx/mbma?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=mbma) **-5.00%** and
  [$MDKA](https://sectors.app/idx/mdka?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=mdka) **-4.81%**, both metals miners.
- A sector-level read would have shown Basic Materials as roughly flat and told you nothing.
  The split is between what the companies actually do, not the label they carry.
- Energy proper moved with the processors, not the miners:
  [$MEDC](https://sectors.app/idx/medc?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=medc) **+6.82%** and [$DEWA](https://sectors.app/idx/dewa?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=dewa)
  **+7.64%** are both Oil, Gas & Coal.

### Foreign money turned on BBCA on Thursday

![Daily net foreign flow for BBCA, 6-10 July 2026: +84B, +13B, +69B, then -141B and -182B](chart-bbca-foreign-flow.svg)

- Foreign investors bought [$BBCA](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-07-13&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) on Monday, Tuesday and
  Wednesday for a combined **IDR 166.48B**.
- They then sold **IDR 322.98B** across Thursday and Friday, closing the week at
  **IDR 156.50B net out**.
- Friday alone was IDR 182.38B out, the single largest print of the week in either direction.
- The reversal is corroborated by the market-wide tape: press reports put Friday's total
  foreign net selling at IDR 421.5B, led by BBCA (see Headlines).
