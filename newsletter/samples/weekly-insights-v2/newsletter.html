What the Data Unearthed
Basic Materials filled both ends of the table
• Four of the ten biggest LQ45 movers share one sector classification, Basic Materials, and they sit at opposite ends of it.
• Up: $BRPT +9.09% and $ESSA +6.48%, both petrochemical processors.
• Down: $MBMA -5.00% and $MDKA -4.81%, both metals miners.
• A sector-level read would have shown Basic Materials as roughly flat and told you nothing. The split is between what the companies actually do, not the label they carry.
• Energy proper moved with the processors, not the miners: $MEDC +6.82% and $DEWA +7.64% are both Oil, Gas & Coal.
Foreign money turned on BBCA on Thursday
• Foreign investors bought $BBCA on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday for a combined IDR 166.48B.
• They then sold IDR 322.98B across Thursday and Friday, closing the week at IDR 156.50B net out.
• Friday alone was IDR 182.38B out, the single largest print of the week in either direction.
• Corroborated by the market-wide tape: press reports put Friday's total foreign net selling at IDR 421.5B, led by BBCA (see Headlines).
Four insiders bought CUAN, and the January buyers are 63% underwater
• Petrindo Jaya Kreasi ($CUAN) closed the week at IDR 620, unchanged across Thursday and Friday.
• Prajogo Pangestu bought 24.30M shares for IDR 41.89B across five January filings, at IDR 1,552 to IDR 1,871. Against Friday's close that position is 63% underwater.
• Three more buys landed at today's prices instead: Michael at IDR 580 on 1 July, Erwin Ciputra at IDR 540 on 6 July, inside this week, and Kartika Hendrawan at IDR 620 on 9 July.
• Same name, two cohorts. The January cluster paid roughly 2.7x what the July cluster paid.
• The card scopes to three insiders and omits Michael's 1 July purchase, which the filings data does show. Every other figure on it reconciles with the API.
