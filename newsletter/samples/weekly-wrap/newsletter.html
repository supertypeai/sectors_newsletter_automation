The week in one line

Across the five sessions from 29 Jun to 3 Jul, IDX total market cap rose +1.02% to IDR 10,283.56T, recovering +3.94% off Tuesday's low of IDR 9,894.03T. The large caps led: LQ45 +1.53%, IDX30 +1.19%. Yet foreign investors were net sellers of Bank Rakyat Indonesia ($BBRI) on every one of the five days, a net IDR 979B out of one name. The tape turned up on domestic flow while the largest bank kept bleeding foreign money.

Index & market

The week was V-shaped: down hard on Tuesday, then four straight sessions higher into Friday's close. That reverses the prior week (22-26 Jun), which fell -3.83% Monday to Friday.

What actually traded

Volume did not follow the blue chips. The most-active list was dominated by low-priced Bakrie-group and coal-adjacent names, led by Bumi Resources ($BUMI), which topped the daily volume table on four of the five sessions (peak 2.68B shares on 30 Jun). Bakrie & Brothers ($BNBR), Bumi Resources Minerals ($BRMS) and Darma Henwa ($DEWA) filled out the rest. BUMI's own foreign flow was roughly flat on the week (about +IDR 28B net), so its churn was a domestic-liquidity story, not a foreign-inflow one.

Headlines
06 Jul '26
IHSG rallies for a fourth day as foreign investors net-buy ten leading Indonesian stocks

The IHSG composite extended its rally to a fourth straight session on Monday, up 0.69% to 5,916.07, near the 6,000 level on Rp 9.5T of turnover. Foreign investors were net sellers of Rp 190.9B market-wide, but net buying was led by BBCA (Rp 168.1B), Chandra Asri (Rp 110.1B) and Bumi Resources Minerals (Rp 75.8B).

Source: kontan.co.id
06 Jul '26
Bank Tabungan Negara accelerates its shift from mortgage specialist to a "beyond-mortgage" bank

BTN is broadening beyond mortgages into integrated financial services and digital ecosystems. It reported consolidated net profit of Rp 1.85T for the period to May 2026, up 54.37% year-on-year, with credit up 9.97% to Rp 403.06T and deposits up 9.09% to Rp 433.95T, outpacing industry growth.

Source: kontan.co.id  |  $BBTN
06 Jul '26
Undisbursed bank loans remain high even as credit expands

Bank Indonesia data show industry credit grew 11.51% year-on-year to May 2026, while undisbursed facilities reached Rp 2,576T, or 22.41% of the credit ceiling. BCA's credit rose 4.85% to Rp 969.09T with its undisbursed book up 6.61% to Rp 360.59T; the bank targets 8-10% credit growth by year-end.

Source: kontan.co.id  |  $BBCA
06 Jul '26
BI rate increase may pressure multifinance bond issuance

With Bank Indonesia's benchmark rate at 5.75%, multifinance bond issuance is expected to turn more selective as investors demand higher coupons. Pefindo notes issuance to June 2026 reached Rp 12.93T, about 32.3% below the full-year 2025 run-rate of Rp 38.18T, with roughly Rp 17.73T of refinancing needs in 2H 2026.

Source: kontan.co.id
06 Jul '26
Foreign investors keep net-buying Bank Central Asia, the day's largest inflow

Foreign investors net-bought BBCA worth Rp 168.1B on Monday, the largest single-stock inflow on the exchange, ahead of Chandra Asri (Rp 110B). Bank Mandiri and Mitra Adiperkasa saw net sells. Market-wide foreign selling of Rp 190.9B brought year-to-date foreign net outflows to Rp 74.6T.

Source: investor.id  |  $BBCA
