Latest Release
The Straits (3.7.0)

The Straits release brings a major expansion to Sectors' coverage of Singapore and Indonesia, with dedicated group pages for major conglomerates, a tripling of Singapore coverage, and the launch of interactive onboarding quests for new users.

What shipped in The Straits
  • Singapore & Indonesia Groups: 80+ dedicated pages for major conglomerates and business groups across both markets, each mapping ownership structure, listed affiliates, and cross-holdings.
  • 3x Singapore coverage expansion: a redesigned SGX overview page, wider company-financials and API coverage, 30 prominent Singapore groups, a 22-name trade ideas page, and 6 new Singapore sector listings.
  • Onboarding quests: new interactive quests walk first-time users through the platform while paying out API credits along the way, paid subscribers can earn an extra 500 credits and Sectors merchandise for finishing them.

Feature Highlight
Conglomerates in SG & ID

One feature from this release is the new conglomerate group pages. Large conglomerates are a defining feature of Southeast Asia's public markets, the new group pages break down a conglomerate's stake in every affiliate it controls, is affiliated with, or holds a position in, algorithmically deduced from Sectors' own Company Ownership data.

How to use it: select a Singapore Group or Indonesia Group from the list. It then brings you to the group page that shows its portfolio's stakes ranked by ownership percentage, followed by a Portfolio & Affiliations section per affiliate.

See it on real data, the Salim Group page from the release note itself: 20 listed companies in Indonesia, all 20 with tracked ownership.
