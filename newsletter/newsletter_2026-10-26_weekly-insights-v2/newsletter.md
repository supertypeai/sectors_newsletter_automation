## Summary

**The bull read**

- The rebound reached nearly the whole LQ45 and the largest gains sat in state controlled banks and telecoms, which gives the recovery a local bid that does not wait for foreign flows to return.
- Issuer buybacks supply demand while the reserve requirement cut supplies bank liquidity, two domestic sources that stay active into November.
- [**BMRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bmri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bmri) and [**AKRA**](https://sectors.app/idx/akra?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=akra) both reported double digit profit growth, which fits a selloff that priced governance risk while earnings kept growing.

**The bear read**

- Market capitalization closed the week well short of its level before the MSCI proposal, so most of the drawdown remains open.
- Foreign selling stayed concentrated in the largest banks even on the strongest rebound day, which leaves benchmark constituents like [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) exposed to the MSCI outcome.
- A tighter auto-reject floor, IPO conduct sanctions and index exclusions reshape liquidity and ownership in the concentrated names, so price discovery there changes whatever MSCI decides.

**What to watch next**

Monitor [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) and [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) price action and volume into the 2 November change in JCI composition:

- If both banks hold their Friday levels on steady volume as the concentrated names leave the composite, it supports the bull read that domestic demand is carrying the benchmark.
- If heavy volume and renewed foreign net selling return to [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) and [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-10-26&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) around that date, it adds weight to the bear read that global funds remain the marginal price setters.
