## What the Data Unearthed

### [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=brpt) and [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=isat) fell about 20% in seven days after reporting growth

- [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=isat) reported nine-month net profit up **37.20%** to IDR 4.92T on 28 October, and the shares fell **6.83%** that day and another **6.69%** the next, closing at IDR 1,465.
- Volume says the selling was heavy: [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=isat) traded 91.85M shares on 28 October, more than twice the 38.71M of 23 October.
- [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=brpt) published nine-month revenue up **56.3%** to USD 8.69B on 30 October, after the shares had already lost **20.14%** in seven days, so the report landed after the market had already marked the shares down.

### [**ENRG**](https://sectors.app/idx/enrg?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=enrg) fell 18.40% in seven days with three straight auto-reject closes

- [**ENRG**](https://sectors.app/idx/enrg?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=enrg) closed at the auto-reject floor on 27, 28 and 29 October, ending at IDR 665 from IDR 815 on 22 October, in the same week it reported nine-month net profit up **30.8%** to USD 72.81M.
- Earnings quality improved underneath the fall: operating margin rose to **37%** from 30% as the average oil price climbed 33.1% to USD 94.92 a barrel.
- Volume on the three floor closes was 205M, 149M and 236M shares, all below the 331M of 22 October, which says sellers outnumbered buyers at the floor and few trades cleared.

### [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) closed the week down 1.69% after a 7.65% jump and a 6.35% reversal in back-to-back sessions

- IDX announced on 28 October that [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) leaves the short-selling list, and the shares rose **7.65%** that session to IDR 5,275, a move whose timing lines up with the removal of short pressure.
- Volume on 28 October reached 583.70M shares, about 59% above the previous session's 366.85M, which shows the rise drew real participation.
- The gain reversed the next day, with a **6.35%** fall to IDR 4,940 on 312.44M shares, which left [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) 1.69% below the prior Friday while the LQ45 lost 5.81%.

## Summary

**The bull read**

- [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) fell far less than the benchmark after leaving the short-selling list, which shows that removing short pressure can steady an index heavyweight while the rest of the market keeps sliding.
- [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=isat) and [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=brpt) sold off through strong growth reports, which points to flows and index-status risk setting prices in large names while earnings take a back seat.

**The bear read**

- Market capitalization closed below its level on the 16 October crash day, so the prior week's rebound is fully erased and the drawdown has resumed.
- The rupiah's record low adds currency loss to price loss for offshore holders, so exits can continue even in names with growing earnings such as [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=isat).
- [**ENRG**](https://sectors.app/idx/enrg?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=enrg)'s floor closes on thin volume show that a 7% auto-reject limit stretches a sell-off across several sessions in names with no bid.

- If [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-02&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) holds its Thursday level on steady volume as the composition change takes effect, it supports the bull read that index heavyweights keep a domestic bid.
