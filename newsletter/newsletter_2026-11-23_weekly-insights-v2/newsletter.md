## What the Data Unearthed

### LQ45 fell 2.66% and total market cap fell 3.56%, with mining names leading the drop

- The broader market gave back all of last week's gain while LQ45 kept part of it: total market cap closed 0.21% below its 6 November level, and LQ45 stayed 3.37% above it.
- Mining names set the pace: [**CUAN**](https://sectors.app/idx/cuan?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=cuan), [**INDY**](https://sectors.app/idx/indy?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=indy), [**MDKA**](https://sectors.app/idx/mdka?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=mdka), [**DEWA**](https://sectors.app/idx/dewa?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=dewa) and [**AMMN**](https://sectors.app/idx/ammn?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=ammn) make up five of the eight weakest LQ45 constituents, and the week opened with President Prabowo Subianto calling for the state to take at least 70% of mining profits.
- Earnings did not hold the prices up, since [**INDY**](https://sectors.app/idx/indy?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=indy) reported sharply higher nine-month profit and fell **-22.40%**, and [**MDKA**](https://sectors.app/idx/mdka?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=mdka) swung to a nine-month profit and fell **-17.79%**, which puts policy risk ahead of earnings in how the week was priced.

### [**WIFI**](https://sectors.app/idx/wifi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=wifi) fell 7.91% in the week one holder sold its entire 26.67% and another took its stake past 50%

- Sinergi Graha Kapital exited its whole 26.67% holding on 19 November, and Investasi Sukses Bersama lifted its own from 27.28% to 54.42% the same day, which gives one holder majority control of [**WIFI**](https://sectors.app/idx/wifi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=wifi).
- The 1.47 billion shares bought were about 23 times the 64.8 million [**WIFI**](https://sectors.app/idx/wifi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=wifi) traded that day, consistent with the stake changing hands outside the regular order book.
- Volume reached 309.2 million shares on Friday, about five times Thursday's, and the price still slipped **-1.21%** to IDR 815, so the heavy turnover found no follow-through bid.

### The four big banks finished within 1% of flat while LQ45 fell 2.66%

- [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) closed up **+0.91%**, [**BBNI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbni?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbni) up **+0.85%**, [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbri) up **+0.65%** and [**BMRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bmri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bmri) unchanged, which made the banks the steadiest large weights in an index that lost ground every day from Tuesday.
- Bank Indonesia raised its policy rate 25 bps to 6% on 18 November and the day split the group, with [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbca) up **+4.55%** while [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=bbri) fell **-1.94%**, which says the market priced the hike bank by bank.
- The weakness reached beyond mining and banks, with [**ASII**](https://sectors.app/idx/asii?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=asii) down **-3.64%** and [**ISAT**](https://sectors.app/idx/isat?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=data-unearthed&utm_term=isat) down **-8.00%**, so the LQ45 decline touched conglomerates and telecoms as well as miners.

## Summary

**The bull read**

- The big banks held their ground while the index fell, which says the heaviest weights in LQ45 still attract buyers when sentiment turns and gives the benchmark a floor the mining names did not supply.
- Bank Indonesia paired its rate hike with a lower reserve requirement, which leaves lenders such as [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) and [**BMRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bmri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bmri) more room to keep funding loans at the higher policy rate.
- Profits kept growing at [**INDY**](https://sectors.app/idx/indy?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=indy) and [**MDKA**](https://sectors.app/idx/mdka?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=mdka), so the mining sell-off sits on a stable earnings base and responds to a policy debate that is still open.

**The bear read**

- A record-low rupiah and an open door to another BI Rate rise in December keep funding costs for banks such as [**BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbca) and [**BBRI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbri?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=bbri) pointing up, which keeps pressure on the index's heaviest sector.
- The state's claim on mining profits, a possible state equity stake and a single export channel remain proposals, so the risk premium on [**CUAN**](https://sectors.app/idx/cuan?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=cuan), [**INDY**](https://sectors.app/idx/indy?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=indy) and [**MDKA**](https://sectors.app/idx/mdka?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=mdka) stays until the terms are known.
- Mechanical selling is still ahead, with the MSCI exit of [**BRPT**](https://sectors.app/idx/brpt?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-11-23&utm_content=other-side&utm_term=brpt) on 30 November and the FTSE Russell deletions on 21 December, so passive outflows can add to a market already down on the week.
