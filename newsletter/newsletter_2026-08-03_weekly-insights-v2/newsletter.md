# The index closed the week at a high while foreign money left

Domestic buying absorbed a market-wide IDR 1.34T foreign net sell this week, and the
split showed up clearest in two Basic Materials names that moved in opposite
directions on opposite foreign flows.

**Issue #31 | 3 August 2026 | week of 27-31 July | data as of 31 July 2026**

## Key Data Bites

*The index closed the week at its own high even though foreign investors were net
sellers of IDR 1.34T, so domestic money did the buying.*

- IDX total market cap closed at **IDR 10,919.63T**, up **+0.48% WoW**, less than half
  the prior week's +1.13%, and it closed the week at its own high after a Wednesday
  trough of IDR 10,671.93T.
- **LQ45 +1.05%** and **IDX30 +1.09%** both beat the whole market's +0.48%, so large
  caps outran the broader tape again.
- LQ45 breadth was **29 up, 11 down**, five unchanged, of 45 constituents.
- [**$INKP**](https://sectors.app/idx/inkp?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-08-03&utm_content=key-data-bites&utm_term=inkp) was the best LQ45 name at **+9.45%**, closing at IDR 8,400.
- [**$UNTR**](https://sectors.app/idx/untr?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-08-03&utm_content=key-data-bites&utm_term=untr) was the worst at **-6.08%**, closing at IDR 23,950.
- [**$BUMI**](https://sectors.app/idx/bumi?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-08-03&utm_content=key-data-bites&utm_term=bumi) and [**$BNBR**](https://sectors.app/idx/bnbr?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-08-03&utm_content=key-data-bites&utm_term=bnbr) placed in the daily top three by volume on **all five sessions**.
- Foreign investors were net sellers market-wide of **IDR 1.34T** this week (exchange
  definition), even as the index closed at a weekly high.
- [**$BBCA**](https://sectors.app/idx/bbca?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly-insights-v2_2026-08-03&utm_content=key-data-bites&utm_term=bbca) drew the single biggest net foreign buy of any name this week, **IDR 431.22B**,

## The Other Side

*Both reads are built from the same two facts, a foreign net sell and an index
closing at a weekly high, argued in opposite directions.*

**The bull read**

- The index closed the week at its own high, IDR 10,919.63T on Friday, after a
  Wednesday trough of IDR 10,671.93T, a genuine within-week recovery rather than a
  single-day pop.
- LQ45 (+1.05%) and IDX30 (+1.09%) both outran the whole market's +0.48%, and
  large-cap breadth was solidly positive at 29 up against 11 down.
- Two of the week's five biggest LQ45 gainers, $INCO and $BRPT, backed their price
  moves with real foreign buying and reported earnings growth, and $TINS, outside the
  LQ45 table entirely, backed a +10.79% week with the market's second-largest net
  foreign buy.

**The bear read**

- Foreign investors were net sellers market-wide of IDR 1.34T this week even as the
  index rose, meaning domestic buying, not foreign inflows, carried the tape.
- $ANTM, in the identical Metals & Minerals industry as gainer $INCO, fell -3.68%
  against the largest net foreign sell of any name in this issue, IDR 202.99B out, and
  $UNTR closed the week as the worst LQ45 performer at -6.08%.
- Bank Indonesia's governor resigned two years early on 27 July, and S&P Global
  Ratings has flagged the transition as a source of monetary-policy uncertainty ahead
  of the 18-19 August rate decision, precisely when $BBCA, this week's single biggest
  net foreign buy, and the banking sector have the most riding on the outcome.

**What would settle it**: BI's 18-19 August RDG is the first real test of whether the
post-Perry-Warjiyo transition unsettles the rate path investors have been pricing in.
A steady policy hold, alongside a benign July CPI print due the morning this issue
goes out, would support the bull case; a surprise move or a hawkish tone from the RDG
would support the bear case.
